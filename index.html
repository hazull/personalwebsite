Visit Official School Publication Page ↗

The Beginning

I never actually joined any organization at school before, so I was really new to the system. There were a lot of works and I was a bit overwhelmed. I actually thought about quitting before because of stress! But anyways, it all started with our Grade 11 subject teacher, Miss Angelica Pesito. She is my favorite teacher EVER. Magaling magturo at engaged kaming lahat sa kaniya. Siya rin ang adviser ng Ang Tinghoy, ang Filipino school publication namin. Sumali ako dahil na rin sa friend kong si Sharil, at fortunately nakapasok naman ako.

The Best Part of High School

I felt a lot of emotions in that publication, pero 'di ko matatanggi na 'yon ang pinakamasayang parte ng high school life ko. Ang dami kong natutunan — pumunta sa ibang lugar to cover, magbabad sa araw, magpabalik-balik to get info, lumakas ang loob mag-interview, mag-technical, mag-layout, mag-edit ng video, at mag-lead.

Becoming EIC & Challenges

Sadly, umalis si Ma'am ang school year na naging EIC ako, and everything felt so hard for me. Very inexperienced ako tapos nawala pa 'yung reason kung bakit ako sumali. Nawala pa 'yung passion ko sa pagsusulat nung time na 'yon, kaya hirap na hirap akong magsulat kahit simpleng article. Almost every time kinakwestyon ko ang pagiging leader ko. There were times na umiiyak ako sa pressure, especially nung lumaban kami sa Division Schools Press Conference.

The Victory

I had to sacrifice my time and effort. I was able to finish the newspaper (shoutout kay Cath diyan!), and we placed and qualified for Regionals. After being the last placed group last time, nakuha namin ang 3rd Place! T-T

Our category was Online Publication — we had to publish a website made from WordPress, make it beautiful, and input editorial cartoons and articles (news, sports, editorial, opinion) all under 4 hours! (Fun fact: the quiet cartoonist of our group, Ronel Benson, is actually my classmate this year and we're closer now!)

Grateful Heart

Kahit napakaraming challenges, ang daming ganap at taong naging special sa akin na nagpasaya ng karanasan ko sa Tinghoy. Kung bibigyan ako ng chance ma-experience uli 'yon, I would never decline it.
